Privacy Policy for AuqaLife

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

AuqaLife ("we", "us", or "our") is an aquaculture management application designed to help shrimp farmers, farm owners, technicians, consultants, and aquaculture professionals manage farming activities and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how AuqaLife collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AuqaLife, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication information
  • Profile information provided by you

AuqaLife may support authentication through email/password and Google Sign-In.

Farm and Pond Information

You may voluntarily enter information related to your aquaculture activities, including:

  • Farm information
  • Pond names and details
  • Pond area and water depth
  • Shrimp stocking information
  • Stocking dates
  • Feed records
  • Water quality records
  • Lime and probiotic management records
  • FRB management information
  • Farming notes and other information entered by you

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your AuqaLife account
  • Authenticate users and provide secure sign-in
  • Store and synchronize your farm and pond records
  • Provide farm management and calculation features
  • Improve application functionality and performance
  • Diagnose technical problems and errors
  • Protect the application against unauthorized access and abuse
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain and improve our services

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Firebase Services

AuqaLife uses services provided by Google Firebase to support authentication, data storage, application functionality, and related services.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase-related security and diagnostic services

Information processed through these services is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and security practices.

Google Privacy Policy

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, AuqaLife may receive basic account information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address, and profile information made available through the authentication process.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate your account and provide access to AuqaLife.

AuqaLife does not receive or store your Google account password.

5. Data Storage

Your account and farming information may be stored using cloud services, including Cloud Firestore.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers when necessary to operate AuqaLife, including cloud infrastructure and authentication providers.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and safety of our users, our service, or others.

7. Your Farm Data

Farm and pond information entered into AuqaLife is provided by the user and is used to provide the application's farming management functionality.

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you enter into the application is accurate and that you have the appropriate rights to provide such information.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain user accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information as described below.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your AuqaLife account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the contact information provided below.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize applicable personal information, subject to legal, security, or legitimate business requirements that may require certain information to be retained.

10. Children's Privacy

AuqaLife is intended for general users involved in aquaculture and farming activities.

The application is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.
